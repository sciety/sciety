# 3b. Configure bonfire ingestion

[Title kept from the original plan. The work changed: we now show posts from several networks on Sciety, not just Bonfire. See [5c](./5c.md).]

We wanted to show public posts about a preprint, from social networks built on open protocols, on that preprint's activity page in Sciety. We built this for four networks: Bluesky and Semble (both use ATProto), and Bonfire and Mastodon (both use ActivityPub).

A "mention" here means a post that links to the preprint by its DOI.

## How it works

Sciety does not copy these posts into its own records. When someone opens a preprint's activity page, Sciety asks the source network for any posts about that preprint and shows them there.

## What we built

**Bluesky** ([#4260](https://github.com/sciety/sciety/issues/4260))

- Posts are found and shown when the page loads. We don't need a list of papers in advance.
- Bluesky mentions are common. You will find them on most eLife reviewed preprints, for example [10.7554/elife.112941.1](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.7554/elife.112941.1).

**Semble** ([#4258](https://github.com/sciety/sciety/issues/4258))

- We asked Semble once for every card that has a DOI, and kept only the DOIs Sciety is likely to support. That gave us 11 papers, for example [10.1101/2024.12.06.627200](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.1101/2024.12.06.627200).
- That list is built into Sciety. When one of those papers' pages loads, Sciety checks Semble for the mention and shows it.
- We kept the list fixed so that we don't overload Semble. Updating it needs a small piece of developer work.
