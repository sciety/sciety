# 3f. Research and experiment with approaches for DocMaps integration

[Devs and Ash to confirm. The below is our understanding from the dev notes, not something the devs have said.]

We have not built a route into DocMaps for posts from social networks.

Sciety retrieves these posts when a page loads and does not store them (see [3b](./3b.md)). So there is nothing in Sciety's own records that could be published as a DocMap yet.

Getting posts into Sciety's records would be the first step, and that needs decisions about how long to keep them and how to remove them.
